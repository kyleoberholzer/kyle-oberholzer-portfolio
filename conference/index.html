13 startups in one room is premium content sitting on the table

You've already done the hard part — getting 13 ocean energy startups into the same building on the same day. Turning that into content that outlasts the event itself is the easy part, and it's sitting right there for the taking.

I bring a compact mini studio and set it up on-site for the day: a proper camera, lighting and mics, run out of a single corner of the venue. Between sessions, each startup sits down for a short, podcast-style interview. No separate shoot days, no chasing founders down after the fact — it all happens inside the day you've already scheduled.

The finished videos live on Oceans of Energy's own channels, not scattered across 13 different personal accounts. Every founder conversation becomes proof of the calibre of company you bring together — content that keeps representing the event long after the doors in The Hague close.
