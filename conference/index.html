Thought leadership conference videos

All your stakeholders in one room is priceless content sitting on the table

You've already done the hard part — getting your stakeholders into the same room on the same day. Turning that into content that outlasts the event itself is the easy part, and it's sitting right there for the taking.

I bring a compact mini studio and set it up on-site for the day: a proper camera, lighting and mics, run out of a single corner of the venue. Between sessions, each stakeholder sits down for a short, podcast-style interview. No separate shoot days, no chasing people down after the fact — it all happens inside the day you've already scheduled.

The finished videos live on your own channels, not scattered across a dozen different personal accounts. Every conversation becomes proof of the calibre of people you bring together — content that keeps representing the event long after the doors close.
