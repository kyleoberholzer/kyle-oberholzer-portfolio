Thought leadership conference videos

All your stakeholders in one room is priceless content sitting on the table

You've already done the hard part: getting all your stakeholders into the same room on the same day. Now is your chance to make content which outlasts the event, drives real engagement, and benefits everyone involved.

I bring a professional mini-studio to your event where stakeholders can sit down for a short, podcast-style interview in between their sessions. And then I turn it into thought leadership style video campaigns which stay relevant for months after.

I start by researching each company individually beforehand so that I can guide a conversation which helps them put their best foot forward. And at the same time I get existing B-roll and photos which they already have.

And then I bring it together in the edit, breaking it down into multiple formats curated for YouTube, LinkedIn and Instagram while focusing on telling a real story which your audience actually cares about.

The resulting videos live on your own channels. Your narrative. Your audience. Your growth.
