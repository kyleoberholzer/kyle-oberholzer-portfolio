For founders

Everyone wants to see what you're building

Your story is your most powerful marketing asset. A documentary-style brand film showing you and your team solving actual problems is proof that the work is real, hard, and matters.

One shoot day is all it takes to get started. And it will do more to attract talent, investors and clients than any slide deck you (and Claude) have ever made before.

My job is to make the conversation feel like a conversation, ask the questions that get past the rehearsed answer, and shape what's left into something people actually want to watch to the end.

Animations. Case studies from the field. A fancy website. These can all be added later (and I can do these for you as well!). But your story is the starting point.

Deep tech nerd. Interviewer. Video content marketer.

Before learning how to grow audiences on the internet I spent years making videos which no-one watched. Because I thought I was just a "filmmaker".
